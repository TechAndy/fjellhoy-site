Receipts

AI for receipts and bookkeeping

Receipts scattered across email and phones slow down bookkeeping. An AI agent can gather them, check invoices and get everything ready for you and your accountant.

Receipts from email

Receipts and invoices arriving by email are picked out and gathered.

  • Receipts taken from the inbox
  • Gathered in one place
  • Missing receipts marked

Invoice checks

New invoices are compared with earlier invoices from the same supplier.

  • Price changes flagged
  • Differences before approval
  • You say yes before anything is paid

Ready for the accountant

The agent makes an overview you can pass on once you have checked it.

  • Monthly overview
  • Deadlines in one place
  • Sent only when you approve

Example: Marte Ådnanes at Lomelde Bygg AS gets a list of missing receipts before month end. She decides what goes to the accountant. The example shows the way of working. It is not a customer story.

The agent does not replace your accountant. It does the groundwork in the accounts you connect.
