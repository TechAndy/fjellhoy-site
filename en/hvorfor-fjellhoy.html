About Fjellhøy

The name

Why the name Fjellhøy?

Fjellhøy is the little hill my grandparents' house sits on. I have had the family dinners there, and every Christmas Eve, for 30 years. It feels safe. Using AI with Fjellhøy should feel the same: no technology talk that makes you feel foolish, just someone who has run a business, helping you — as a manager or a board member — get more out of the day.

– Andreas Berthelsen, managing director

About me

Andreas Berthelsen

AI assistant for small businesses and boards

A hands-on manager, accountable for results at a production company with more than 20 employees. Chair of a condominium with 65 units, and board roles in voluntary organisations. Managing director of his own company, Fjellhøy AS.

I now help small businesses, sole proprietors and boards take up AI in a way that feels safe, and stays simple.
