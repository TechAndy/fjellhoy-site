Email and calendar

Automation of email and calendar

Email and the calendar belong together. An AI agent suggests replies, open times and reminders. You read them and approve them before anything is sent.

The inbox

The agent sorts what has come in, and drafts replies to what can be answered straight away.

  • Important threads marked first
  • Draft replies you can edit
  • Sending happens after approval

Open times

The agent looks at the calendar and suggests times that are free, with a draft invitation.

  • Suggestions taken from the calendar
  • A draft confirmation
  • You approve the invitation before it is sent

Reminders

The agent finds deadlines in email and suggests a calendar entry, so they do not stay buried in a thread.

  • Deadlines pulled out of the email
  • A suggested calendar entry
  • You decide what is added

Example: Marte Ådnanes gets draft replies to email that is waiting, and a suggestion for when the follow-up can sit in the calendar. She approves it before anything is sent.

The agent is set up in your own accounts. Ready in a few hours. The agent subscription is extra, and you pay the vendor directly.
