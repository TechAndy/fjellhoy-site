Email

AI for email and customer follow-up

The inbox and the follow-up don't have to wait until evening. An AI agent sorts what came in, writes the drafts, and finds threads that have gone quiet. You read them before anything goes out.

The inbox when you start

What is urgent can sit at the top. The rest is cleared, so you are not hunting for the thing that matters.

  • Important enquiries at the top
  • The rest cleared out of the way
  • One place to start the day

Draft replies in your tone

The agent writes a draft you can change. It does not send the reply itself.

  • A draft ready to read
  • You change what needs changing
  • Nothing is sent without approval

Customer follow-up

When a quote, a meeting or an enquiry has gone without a reply, the agent can suggest a short follow-up.

  • Threads that have gone quiet
  • A draft reminder
  • You choose what is sent

Example: Marte Ådnanes gets draft replies to email that is waiting, and a suggested follow-up. She approves it before anything is sent. Sindre Vassenden can start the day with the enquiries that are ready. The examples show how the work is done. They are not customer stories.

The setup is done in the email you already use. Ready in a few hours. The calendar can be included where it is needed.
