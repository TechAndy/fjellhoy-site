        In plain terms

        What is an AI agent?

        An AI agent is a digital colleague that uses artificial intelligence to do the work, not only answer questions. The personal assistant I set up for you is that kind of agent. It works in three simple steps:

        1. 1 · Reads

          It only sees what you have chosen to share, for example email, calendar and invoices.

        3. 2 · Thinks

          It works out what is urgent, compares it with what has happened before, and suggests the next step.

        5. 3 · Does

          It sorts, drafts and reminds you of deadlines. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

        You're the boss. The agent does the prep.

        No need to take sides in the AI race

        You are not locked to one AI brand. The platform the assistant runs on picks the model, and it can change that model over time. You don't have to follow any of that, and you don't have to take a side.

        You're in control, the whole time

        Security first

        Security matters more than anything else I set up. Here is what you can count on: plain language, and no promise I can't keep.

        • You choose what it can see

          The agent only reaches the accounts you connect, and you can disconnect them whenever you like.

        • Nothing goes out without your yes

          Email, payments and deletions always wait for your yes. You see the suggestion first.

        • Your own separate account

          The vendor's documentation says each customer gets their own account, on a separate machine. One user cannot reach another user's machine.

        • Your password stays yours

          You sign in, enter one-time codes and make payments yourself. The agent never types your password.

        • You can see what it's doing

          Every suggestion is shown before anything happens, and you get regular summaries of what the agent has done.

        • We go through it when you start

          At the start we go through access and privacy together, including where the data is processed.

        Frequently asked questions

        Can't find the answer? Call +47 416 00 048, or email andreas@fjellhoy.com.

        What do I need?

        The email and calendar you already use, and a few hours for setup. You don't need a technical background. I set it up and show you how to use it on your own work.

        Do I need to be good with technology?

        No. I handle the technical side. You only say yes or no to what it suggests.

        How does getting started work?

        We start with a free call. Then we set the assistant up together, and you approve the access yourself.

        Can I see what the assistant can do for my industry?

        Yes. Under Your example you pick a type and an industry, and you see a sample setup before we talk.

        Is an AI agent the same as ChatGPT?

        No. ChatGPT answers when you ask. An AI agent is set up to work inside your own tools, such as email, calendar and invoices, and to do the regular jobs for you. You approve each one.

        Can the assistant send email or pay without me?

        No. Anything that is sent, paid or deleted needs your yes.

        What can the agent NOT do?

        It only sees the accounts you have connected. It does not log in, and it does not type passwords or payment details for you. You do that yourself. It makes no decisions on your behalf, and it does not replace an accountant or a lawyer. It does the prep. You decide.

        What does the AI platform itself cost?

        The agent subscription sits on top of my prices, and is paid directly to the vendor with your own card. I only invoice setup and follow-up. We go through the subscription price on the free call. The cost drivers, and the vendors' published list prices, are in What an AI agent costs a small business in 2026.

        How do you handle GDPR and a data processing agreement?

        The setup happens in the accounts you already have, and you approve anything before it is sent. We sign a data processing agreement with you before we start. There is more on the page GDPR and data processing agreement.
