Finance department

AI help with invoice intake, prepared vouchers and a monthly report for companies using Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma

The finance department in a company of about 30 to 150 people takes in invoices, codes and posts them, and pulls the monthly report together. Fjellhøy sets up an AI assistant, the same kind of agent described elsewhere on this site, that works alongside the ERP you already use. It prepares suggestions. You or your accountant approve before anything is sent.

Invoice intake

The assistant gathers what has come in, and suggests a match against an order, an agreement or an earlier invoice from the same supplier.

  • A suggested match
  • Exceptions flagged before approval
  • Nothing is sent without approval

Prepared vouchers

It suggests the account, the VAT code and the department from the way similar vouchers were coded before. The suggestion waits for the person who should say yes.

  • A suggested coding
  • Exceptions worth a look
  • You or your accountant approve

The monthly report

The assistant drafts a report you can read: what is missing, and what needs a note. It does not go any further until you have approved it.

  • A draft monthly report
  • Gaps and exceptions in one place
  • Sent only after a yes
Alongside the ERP

The assistant works alongside Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma

The assistant does not replace Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma. The books stay in the system you have. Fjellhøy does not offer a certified integration, a partnership or a ready-made connector for any of them. We set the assistant up in accounts your company owns, and shape it around the routines you describe in the first conversation. What you get are suggestions: coding, matching, exceptions and a draft monthly report. The suggestion is ready, and you or your accountant approve before anything is sent. The point is control.

Does the assistant replace Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma?

No. It works alongside the system you already use. The books stay there. The assistant prepares suggestions, and you or your accountant approve before anything is sent.

Is this a certified integration or a ready-made connector for Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma?

No. Fjellhøy does not offer a certified integration, a partnership or a ready-made connector for Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma. We set the assistant up in accounts your company owns, shaped around the routines you already have.

Can the assistant post entries or send the monthly report on its own?

No. You or your accountant approve before anything is sent. Control stays with the finance department.

What can the assistant suggest?

Account coding, a match against an order, an agreement or an earlier invoice, and exceptions someone should look at before saying yes. It can also draft a monthly report for you to read before it goes any further.

Who is this page for?

The finance department in a company of about 30 to 150 people that takes in invoices, posts them and prepares a monthly report. The page is written for a department like that, for example in trading or distribution, that already uses Xledger, Business Central, Tripletex or Visma.

Where does the data go?

That is settled for each customer and written into the data processing agreement. A data processing agreement is signed before we start. There is more on Security, and on GDPR and the data processing agreement.
