Questions and answers

Common questions about automation for an accounting firm

Can't find the answer? Call +47 416 00 048, or email andreas@fjellhoy.com.

What does the setup do for an accounting firm?

The setup takes the routine work in the inbox: missing documents, payment reminders and draft replies to clients. It works in the accounts the firm already has. You approve it before anything is sent.

Can the agent connect to Outlook, Gmail, an accounting system, time tracking and a calendar?

Yes, to the kind of system you already use: email such as Outlook or Gmail, an accounting system, time tracking or projects, and a calendar. It is not an official partnership with a particular vendor. We set up what fits the firm.

Does the agent send a reminder or ask for documents on its own?

No. It writes the draft and leaves it ready. Someone at the firm reads it and says yes before it is sent.

Does the agent replace the accountant?

No. It does the prep: reminders, drafts and a view of what is waiting. The professional judgement, and what gets booked, stays with you.

Where does client data live?

In the firm's own accounts. Fjellhøy does not copy the email or the documents beyond what the setup requires, and access is removed when the setup is finished. We sign a data processing agreement with you before we start. There is more on GDPR and data processing agreement.
