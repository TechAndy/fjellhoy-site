Estate agents

An AI agent for estate agents

Viewings and interested buyers produce a lot of similar email. The agent drafts the follow-up and the replies. The agent approves nothing. You do.

After a viewing

The agent drafts a follow-up after the viewing, so the buyer gets a reply you have already read.

  • A draft ready after the viewing
  • You adjust the wording
  • It is sent only after you approve it

Questions from buyers

The agent gathers questions about the property and drafts a reply from what has already been said.

  • Enquiries in one place
  • Draft replies to the usual questions
  • You approve it before it goes out

Finding a time

The agent suggests open times for another viewing or a call, and drafts a confirmation.

  • Suggestions taken from the calendar
  • A draft confirmation
  • Nothing is sent without approval

Example: Instefjord Eiendom AS gets a follow-up drafted after a viewing, ready to approve before it is sent to the interested buyer.

The agent is set up in your own accounts. Ready in a few hours. The agent subscription is extra, and you pay the vendor directly.
