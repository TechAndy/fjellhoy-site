AI for board papers

Board work in housing cooperatives, associations and small companies often drowns in paperwork. An AI agent can draft notices, agendas and minutes, and keep track of matters.

Notice and agenda

The agent drafts the notice and agenda from the matters submitted.

  • Draft notice
  • Agenda gathered
  • The chair approves

Draft minutes

After the meeting the agent drafts minutes from the notes, ready for review.

  • Draft from meeting notes
  • Decisions listed
  • Edited before signing

Open matters

Enquiries and matters are followed up so the board sees what is waiting.

  • Overview of open matters
  • Reply drafts for enquiries
  • Sent only when the board says yes

Example: Halvard Mjøsund chairs a board and gets a draft notice a week before the meeting. He corrects it and sends it himself. The example shows the way of working. It is not a customer story.
