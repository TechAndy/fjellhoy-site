Audit trail

Every suggestion the assistant makes, and every yes or no from a person, is logged. The log shows what was suggested, who approved it, and what was left in place. It is there so the finance department, or whoever you name, can go back and see what happened. The log is not a certificate, and it is not a guarantee.

Access control

The assistant gets the least access the task needs, and only for the period you have agreed to. You own the accounts. Fjellhøy does not. You can revoke the access whenever you want. Access is removed when setup is finished, or when the follow-up ends.

Fjellhøy does not ask to keep access after the work is done. You sign in and enter one-time codes yourselves, as the short version on the home page also says.

Where data is stored

We do not name a data centre on this page, and we do not point to a certification. Where data is stored and processed is settled for each customer and written into the data processing agreement.

The setup happens in the accounts you already use. The provider you choose for the assistant itself processes information under the agreement you have with that provider. Fjellhøy does not copy email or documents beyond what the setup requires.

A person approves before anything is sent

The assistant prepares. It does not post, send or delete anything until a person has said yes. That includes a draft monthly report, a reminder, and anything else that would leave the company. Without that yes, nothing happens.

A data processing agreement is signed before we start

A data processing agreement is signed before we start. The text of the agreement is not a file on the website. GDPR and the data processing agreement describes the frame: setup in your own accounts, approval before anything is sent, and a signature before we start. The full privacy notice is on Privacy.

The agreement is not a certification, and it is not a legal guarantee. The GDPR page says the same.

Questions and answers

What is logged?

Every suggestion the assistant makes, and every yes or no from a person. The log shows what was suggested, who approved it, and what was left in place. It is there for your own control. It is not a certification.

Who owns the accounts, and can access be revoked?

You own the accounts. The assistant gets the least access the task needs, and only for as long as you have agreed. You can revoke that access. Access is removed when setup is finished, or when the follow-up ends.

Where is the data stored?

That is settled for each customer and written into the data processing agreement. We do not name a data centre here, and we do not point to a certification.

Can anything be sent before a person has said yes?

No. The assistant prepares. It does not post, send or delete anything until a person has approved it.

Where is the data processing agreement?

A data processing agreement is signed before we start. The text of the agreement is not a file on the website. The page on GDPR and the data processing agreement describes the frame, and the agreement is signed with you before we start.
