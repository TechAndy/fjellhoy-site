Training

AI training for businesses

A course for companies that want to use an AI agent on their own work. You practise drafting and approving. Register your interest, and we will be in touch.

Your own work

The course starts from the work the company already does, so the exercises feel like the working day.

  • An inbox and calendar you already know
  • Tasks taken from the actual business
  • Shaped around that one company

Draft, then approve

You practise the sequence: the agent writes a draft, and a person reads it before it is sent.

  • Draft replies in your own tone
  • A review before anything is sent
  • Nothing goes out without a yes

One shared way of working

The course shows how several people in the company can use the agent the same way, in the same order each time.

  • Draft first, approval second
  • Work stays in your own accounts
  • Questions are taken as you go

Example: Halvard Mjøsund at Brekkevik Rør AS wants several people in the firm to approve the agent's drafts in the same way, and registers interest in a course shaped around the company.
