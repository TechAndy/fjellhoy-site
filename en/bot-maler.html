Bot templates

Four setups for typical tasks

We set up an assistant in the accounts you already use. It prepares the groundwork – checking, sorting and drafting. You approve before anything goes further. Setup of one track: NOK 4,900 ex VAT, including a short training session. Ongoing support is optional, at NOK 790 per month ex VAT. The agent and platform subscription is billed separately by the vendor.

The templates are Fjellhøy's delivery basis for the published setup, not a self-serve product you download. Fjellhøy does not host a separate programme, and does not promise an integration with every specialist system.

The four tracks

Invoice checks

Reads incoming invoices in the inbox or field you point to, compares them with earlier invoices and the contract where it has been added, and flags exceptions for the person who approves payment.

  • Price increases, double billing and unusual items
  • A short recommendation before approval
  • A record of what has been checked

Draft replies to residents

Prepares a first draft reply to common enquiries, in the board's tone, referring to the articles or house rules where those have been added.

  • A draft, not a finished reply
  • The board reads and sends it
  • Suited to condominiums and housing cooperatives

Board-mail sorting

Sorts the board inbox by what is urgent, and prepares a short overview of what is waiting, so items are less likely to sit untouched.

  • Important items marked first
  • Deadlines gathered in one place
  • No email is sent without a yes

Meeting minutes

Prepares a first draft of the minutes from the material the board provides, so the secretary has something to correct instead of starting from a blank page.

  • A first draft, not finished minutes
  • A person reads and adopts them
  • Can be set up on its own or with the other tracks

How the setup works

First a scoping conversation about the task, the accounts and who will approve. A data processing agreement is signed before access is given. You grant access yourself, to the extent the task requires.

  • A trial run on a small, agreed set of cases
  • A short briefing for the person who will approve day to day
  • Fjellhøy's access is removed when the setup is done, or when agreed follow-up ends

Limits

The template does not send, approve, pay or delete without a person's yes. It does not replace a business manager, accountant, auditor, lawyer or building adviser.

  • Quality depends on the material you provide
  • The assistant can miss an exception or use the wrong tone – which is why a person must read first
  • No integration with a specialist system is promised before it has been settled in the scoping

Price for the setup

Setup of one track costs NOK 4,900 ex VAT, including a short training session. Ongoing support is optional, at NOK 790 per month ex VAT. The agent and platform subscription comes in addition and is billed by the vendor. This is the published price on fjellhoy.com, the same as on the front page. This page does not sell a cheaper self-serve template product.

Common questions

Can I buy the templates and set them up myself?

No, not as a self-serve product. The templates are used when Fjellhøy sets up one track in the accounts you already use, at the published setup price.

Do we have to take all four tracks?

No. The setup covers one track at a time. Further tracks can be agreed later, each as its own setup.

Does this replace the business manager or the accountant?

No. The assistant prepares the groundwork. Responsibility and approval stay with you.

What does it cost?
