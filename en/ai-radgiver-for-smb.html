An AI adviser for small businesses

Fjellhøy AS helps the managing director who wants to automate email, invoices and customer follow-up. We set up an AI agent in the tools the company already has. You approve it before anything is sent.

For the person who still does most of it

The setup is for small businesses where the managing director still handles email, invoices and follow-up, and does not want to hire another person for that work.

  • Email and customer follow-up
  • Invoices and deadlines
  • You keep the decision

Independent of any one vendor

There are several large providers. Fjellhøy is not a partner or a reseller for any of them. The job is to make an agent fit the business.

  • A choice based on what you already use
  • Setup in your own accounts
  • Training on your own tasks
