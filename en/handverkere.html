Plumber, electrician, carpenter

For plumbers, electricians and carpenters

A plumber, an electrician and a carpenter share the same evening: a quote that should have gone out, a site visit still to book, and an invoice sitting there. The assistant drafts them while you are on the job.

Example: Halvard Mjøsund at Brekkevik Rør AS gets a quote drafted while he is already on the next job. He reads it and sends it himself. A reminder to a customer such as Lomelde Bygg AS is read before it goes out. The example shows the way of working. It is not a customer story.

The systems you already have

The connections a trades business usually needs

Email: Outlook or Gmail

Calendar

Price

What does the assistant cost per month?

It is not one figure, and there is no number on this page. The monthly cost has two parts, and they are billed separately.

One part is the setup from Fjellhøy. That is a one-off. The price is listed in the pricing section on the home page, along with follow-up if you want adjustments afterwards. We do not repeat the amount here. Ask for a quote, and we will go through what fits your business.

The other part is the platform subscription. The vendor you choose bills that separately. The subscription price is not on this page. It depends on the vendor and how many people need access, and we go through it on the free call.

When someone asks about the price per month, that is the vendor's subscription, and follow-up from Fjellhøy if you want it. Both are settled before we start. See the pricing section, or book a call and ask for a quote.

Common questions about automation for tradespeople

What does the assistant do for a tradesperson?

The assistant drafts quotes, site-visit bookings, time records, payment reminders and a nudge about missing receipts while you are on the job. It works in the accounts you already have. You approve it before anything is sent.

Does this fit a plumber, an electrician and a carpenter?

Yes. A plumber, an electrician and a carpenter share the same pile of paperwork: an enquiry, a site visit, hours and an invoice. The assistant writes the drafts. You read them when you have a quiet minute.

What does the assistant cost per month?

There is no figure on this page. The cost is the one-off setup, listed in the pricing section on the home page, plus the platform subscription, which the vendor bills separately. Ask for a quote, and we will go through the monthly cost on a call.

Can it connect to email, an accounting system, time tracking and a calendar?

Yes, to the kind of system you already use: email such as Outlook or Gmail, an accounting system, time tracking or projects, and a calendar. It is not an official partnership with a particular vendor.

Does it send a quote or a reminder without me?

No. It leaves the draft ready. You say yes before a quote, a payment reminder or a note about missing receipts is sent.
