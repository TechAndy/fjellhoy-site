Housing boards

Less hassle and better control for the board

Board work happens in people’s spare time, but the responsibility is the same. Invoices need approving, residents are waiting for a reply, and deadlines have to be kept. An AI assistant does the groundwork and keeps a record. The board decides.

The assistant works alongside the board portal and the business manager you already have. It does not replace them, and nothing is sent or approved without the board saying yes.

What the assistant takes care of

Checking invoices

The agent checks incoming invoices against the agreement and earlier amounts, and marks anything that looks off.

  • Differences marked before approval
  • A list of invoices waiting for a decision
  • A reminder before the due date

Replies to residents

The agent drafts replies to ordinary questions about maintenance, parking, house rules and voluntary work days.

  • Drafts in the board's own tone
  • Messages sorted by topic
  • Nothing is sent without approval

Board email

The agent sorts the board inbox, keeps deadlines in one place and drafts an agenda.

  • Important items marked first
  • Deadlines gathered in one place
  • A draft agenda and minutes

A record that survives a change of board

What has been checked, answered and decided is kept in order. When the board changes, the history is there – not only in the chair’s head.

  • A trail of what was approved
  • An easier handover to a new board
  • A better basis for the annual meeting

Safe, and under the board’s control

The assistant only sees the accounts the board connects. Nothing is sent, paid or deleted without a yes from the board. We sign a data processing agreement before we start. The assistant does not replace the property manager.

  • The board approves everything
  • Access can be removed at any time
  • Does not replace the property manager

Example: the business client chairs the board. Before each meeting he gets a view of the items, the deadlines and the invoices that need approval, without digging through the inbox.

The agent is set up in your own accounts. Ready in a few hours. The agent subscription is extra, and you pay the vendor directly.

Questions and answers

Common questions

Can’t find the answer? Call +47 416 00 048 or email andreas@fjellhoy.com.

Does this replace the property manager?

No. The property manager keeps the books and makes payments. The assistant helps the board check, reply and keep an overview.

Can the assistant pay or approve invoices?

No. It checks and recommends. The board itself approves and pays.

What about the residents’ privacy?

The assistant works in the board’s own accounts. We go through access and privacy at the start and sign a data processing agreement with the housing co-op or condominium.

What does it cost a board?

Setup from NOK 4,900 ex VAT. Ongoing support is optional, at NOK 790 per month ex VAT. The agent and platform subscription is extra and is billed separately.
