        In plain terms

        What is an AI agent?

        An AI agent is a digital colleague that uses artificial intelligence to do tasks for you, not only answer questions. The personal assistant I set up for you is exactly that kind of agent. It works in three simple steps:

        1. 1 · Reads

          It only sees what you have chosen to give it access to, for example email, calendar and invoices.

        3. 2 · Thinks

          It works out what is urgent, compares it with what has happened before, and suggests the next step.

        5. 3 · Does

          It sorts, writes drafts and reminds you of deadlines. Nothing is sent without your yes.

        You are in charge. The agent does the groundwork.

        You do not have to pick a side in the AI race

        You are not tied to one particular AI brand. The platform the assistant runs on chooses which model is used, and it can change model over time. You do not have to keep up with that. You do not have to pick a side.

        You stay in control

        Security first

        Security is the most important part of everything I set up. Here is what you can count on, without big words and without promises I cannot keep.

        • You decide the access

          The agent only gets access to the accounts you choose to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time.

        • Nothing without your yes

          I set it up so that email, payments and deletions always wait for your yes. You see the suggestion first.

        • A separate account of your own

          According to the vendor's documentation, each customer gets their own account, with a separate machine. One user cannot reach another user's machine.

        • Your password stays yours

          You handle login, one-time codes and payments yourself. The agent does not type your password.

        • You see what happens

          Every suggestion is shown to you before it is carried out, and you get regular summaries of what the agent has done.

        • A review when you start

          When you start, we go through access and privacy together, including where the data is processed.

        Frequently asked questions

        Can't find the answer? Call +47 416 00 048 or email andreas@fjellhoy.com.

        What do I need?

        Just the email and calendar you already use, and a few hours for setup. You do not need a technical background. I set everything up and show you how to use it on your own work.

        Do I need to be good with technology?

        No. I handle the technical side. You only say yes or no to the suggestions.

        How does getting started work?

        We start with a free call. Then we set up the assistant together, and you approve the access yourself.

        Can I see what the assistant can do for my industry?

        Yes. Under Your example you choose a type and an industry, and you see an example setup before we talk.

        Is an AI agent the same as ChatGPT?

        No. ChatGPT answers when you ask. An AI agent is set up to work in your own tools, such as email, calendar and invoices, and to do regular tasks for you, with you approving them.

        Can the assistant send email or pay without me?

        No. Anything that is sent, paid or deleted needs your yes.

        What can the agent NOT do?

        It only sees the accounts you have connected, and it does not log in or type passwords and payment details for you. You do that yourself. It makes no decisions on your behalf, and it does not replace an accountant or a lawyer. It does the groundwork. You decide.

        What does the AI platform itself cost?

        The agent subscription is on top of my prices and is paid directly to the vendor with your own card. I only invoice setup and follow-up. We go through the subscription price on the free call. The cost drivers, and the vendors' published list prices, are in What an AI agent costs a small business in 2026.
