You're on the job – the assistant handles the paperwork

Quotes, site visits, hours, receipts and reminders often wait until the evening. An AI assistant drafts them while you are on the job, and keeps track of what is waiting. You review and hit send.

What the assistant takes care of

Quote emails

The agent drafts quotes from your notes, and follows up with customers who have not replied.

  • A quote drafted from notes or photos
  • Follow-up on quotes that went quiet
  • You approve it before it is sent

Booking a site visit

The agent suggests times from your calendar, and drafts a confirmation once the customer has chosen.

  • Suggestions for open times
  • A confirmation and a reminder for the customer
  • It lands in your calendar

Invoice follow-up

The agent keeps track of invoices you have sent, and drafts a reminder when one falls due.

  • A view of unpaid invoices
  • A reminder draft when something is due
  • A note when the customer has paid

Price

What does the assistant cost per month?

The price has two parts. You pay a one-off setup fee to Fjellhøy, and a platform subscription direct to the vendor. On top of that you can choose ongoing support by the month. All three are set out below, with a worked example for a company with 15 employees. All Fjellhøy prices are excl. VAT.

Prices at Fjellhøy

  • Setup: NOK 4 900 one-off, excl. VAT.
  • Ongoing support: NOK 790 per month, excl. VAT. Optional.

The platform subscription comes in addition and is paid direct to the vendor.

Worked example for a company with 15 employees

The example applies to a company with 10–30 employees. The managing director and one person in the office use the assistant, so two user licences. Everyone else carries on as before.

  • Setup at Fjellhøy: NOK 4 900 one-off.
  • Platform, two users: 2 × USD 18 × 9.23 = NOK 332.28 per month, about NOK 332. Twelve months comes to NOK 3 987.36.
  • Ongoing support, if you want it: NOK 790 per month, which is NOK 9 480 over twelve months.

The platform figure is the vendor's list price for a Microsoft 365 Copilot Business add-on, USD 18 per user per month. It was read on 21 October 2026 and converted at 9.23, a mid-market rate from CurrencyBeacon on 4 November 2026. That is a conversion, not a price the vendor has quoted in Norway, and other vendors charge differently. The add-on also requires a qualifying Microsoft 365 Business subscription, and the campaign price applies to the first year only. We go through which platform suits you on the call.

What the first year costs

  • Without ongoing support: NOK 4 900 + (12 × NOK 332.28) = NOK 8 887.36, about NOK 8 900.
  • With ongoing support: NOK 4 900 + (12 × NOK 332.28) + (12 × NOK 790) = NOK 18 367.36, about NOK 18 400. Ongoing support is optional and billed by the month, so all twelve months are included here.

The final price depends on the vendor, the number of users, and whether the company already has an agreement. The rate is a mid-market figure from CurrencyBeacon on 4 November 2026, and an invoice in kroner may come out differently once the vendor applies its own rate.

Start with one user

The assistant can be set up on email and calendar first. That lets a company begin with one user and add more as the tasks grow. With one user the platform is USD 18 × 9.23 = NOK 166.14 per month, about NOK 166, on the same terms as above.
