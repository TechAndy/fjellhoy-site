Construction

An AI agent for construction firms

Customers want to know what is happening, including when people are on site. The agent drafts the updates. Someone at the firm approves them before they are sent.

A status for the customer

The agent writes a short draft on where the work stands, which you can send after reading it.

  • A draft status update
  • Ready to read through
  • It is sent only after approval

A change in the schedule

When something moves, the agent drafts an explanation of the change for the customer.

  • The change written as a draft
  • You correct the details
  • Nothing is sent without approval

Site visits

The agent suggests a time from the calendar and drafts a confirmation.

  • A suggested time
  • A draft confirmation
  • You approve it before it is sent

Example: Lomelde Bygg AS gets a short update drafted when a site visit has been moved, and approves the text before the customer receives it.

The agent is set up in your own accounts. Ready in a few hours. The agent subscription is extra, and you pay the vendor directly.
